01 · Document processing

ActiveFAX

A fax intake platform redesigned so staff triage, verify and route documents with less effort.

Client
HealthWare Systems
Role
Product design, end to end
Platform
Web app + admin console
Scope
Audit, system, redesign

Challenge

Four typefaces, about fifteen competing colours, a header repeated on every screen and hard-to-read charts, in a tool staff use all day.

Approach

Audit first, then five principles and a token system: one typeface, one accent where colour means status, left navigation, quiet tables, document-first review.

Delivered

Sign-in and 2FA, dashboard, inbound and outbound queues, OCR-highlighted review, audit trail and a 31-screen admin console.

02 · Engagement platform

GamifyPRO

Turning training, goals and peer recognition into a game that teams want to come back to.

Product
GamifyPRO
Role
Product design
Platform
Web app, player + admin
Scope
Redesign, admin, brand + email

Challenge

Required training, goals and recognition feel like chores, so people do the minimum. The product had to make progress visible and worth chasing.

Approach

A clear game loop: quests earn XP and coins, XP moves you along a rank road from Rookie to Hall of Fame, and coins spend in a rewards shop. Streaks, leaderboards and kudos keep people returning, and every win gets its own moment with the next goal in view.

Delivered

Player app from "Press Start" sign-in and player setup to command center, quest board and detail, completion and level-up moments, achievements, awards vault and rank road, plus team management, award assignment, email templates and a brand guide.

E1 Pulse suite

Dashboards that show hospitals how many uninsured patients were screened, enrolled and paid for.

Client
HealthWare Systems
Role
Product design
Platform
Web portal, PDF reports, admin tool
Scope
4 products, light + dark

Challenge

Hospital finance teams need to see millions of dollars of patient accounts move from screening to coverage, filtered by facility, contract and program, and share it as a report.

Client

Challenge

Setting up cameras and smart locks is where people give up, and in an emergency nobody should hunt for the right button.

How an appgets designed

Five phases on every project. The depth changes; the order doesn't.

01

Discover

  • Stakeholder interviews
  • User interviews and shadowing
  • Audit of the current product
  • Data and support review

Output · audit and problem statements

02

Define

  • Journeys and task flows
  • Information architecture
  • Design principles
  • Success measures

Output · flows, IA map, principles

03

Design

  • Wireframes
  • Tokens and components
  • Interactive prototypes
  • High-fidelity UI

Output · Figma library and screens

04

Validate

  • Usability testing
  • Stakeholder reviews
  • Accessibility checks
  • Iteration

Output · findings and revisions

05

Deliver

  • Specs and redlines
  • Developer pairing
  • Front-end in HTML/CSS
  • Build QA

Output · handoff and shipped UI
